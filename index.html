About Me

I'm a game producer with a foundation in mechanical engineering, and I design and manage games from ideation to launch, including Web3 games. I handle account management, contract creation, budget tracking, and milestone tracking across projects. I've worked across hypercasual and hybrid-casual development as well as iGaming, with a focus on cross-functional collaboration and data-informed design. I'm currently pursuing a PhD exploring affective computing and machine learning in game design, bringing an engineer's rigor to how games are built and understood.
